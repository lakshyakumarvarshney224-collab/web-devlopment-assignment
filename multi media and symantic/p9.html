Welcome to Virat Kohli Sports Academy

Welcome to Virat Kohli Sports Academy, a sports training portal focused on cricket, fitness, discipline and professional development.

Our training programs help young athletes improve their skills, fitness and understanding of the game.

Coach Profiles

Head Cricket Coach

The head coach focuses on batting, bowling, fielding and match preparation.

Fitness Coach

The fitness coach helps athletes improve strength, stamina, flexibility and overall fitness.

Junior Cricket Coach

The junior coach provides basic cricket training and helps young players develop their fundamentals.

Training Steps

  1. Register for the academy.
  2. Select a suitable training program.
  3. Attend the fitness assessment.
  4. Attend regular cricket practice.
  5. Participate in practice matches.
  6. Take part in tournaments.
