Welcome to National Tech & Career Expo

Welcome to the National Tech & Career Expo 2026. This event brings together students, technology companies, professionals, trainers and industry experts.

Participants can explore career opportunities, internships, workshops, placements, certifications and networking opportunities.

Date: 15 November 2026

Venue: Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh

About the Expo

Event Introduction

The expo provides students and job seekers with an opportunity to meet companies, attend workshops and learn about modern technology careers.

Organizer Message

Our goal is to connect talented students with leading organizations and help them understand future career paths.

Education, technology and networking can create better opportunities for the next generation.

Important Information

Participants should carry their college ID card and resume.

Entry to selected workshops may require prior registration.
