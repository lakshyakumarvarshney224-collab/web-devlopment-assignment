CAMPUS

GLA University's Greater Noida campus features a modern, tech-enabled infrastructure with air-conditioned smart classrooms, advanced practical laboratories, high-speed Wi-Fi, specialized sports amenities, and safe, hygienic hostel accommodations.

ADMISSION PROCESS

Admissions at GLA University's Greater Noida campus are managed entirely online through a structured process. Candidates must first complete the online registration form and pay the non-refundable registration fee via the official university portal.
Eligible applicants then schedule and appear for the mandatory two-hour online GLA Entrance Test (GLAET) or present valid national exam scores like JEE Main for BTech or CAT/MAT for MBA. Shortlisted candidates undergo a personal interview round, and final seat allocation is confirmed upon successful document verification and token academic fee payment
