Data in Our DNA

INTRODUCTION

Anagha Rajesh, a 24-year-old chemical engineer, founded the deep-tech startup BioCompute to encode digital data directly into synthetic DNA molecules. After developing an end-to-end prototype and reducing experimental storage costs in Bengaluru, the startup relocated to San Francisco to access long-term venture capital.

TIMES OF INDIA ARTICLE

What

The core innovation involves transforming digital binary code into the biological language of DNA using four chemical bases, which is then manufactured into synthetic strands.
