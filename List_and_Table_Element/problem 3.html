Before Practical Class

Students should follow these steps:

  1. Read the practical topic.
  2. Complete the given theory work.
  3. Bring the practical notebook.
  4. Attend the practical class on time.
  5. Complete the practical task.

Classroom Rules

  • Attend classes regularly.
  • Be present on time.
  • Bring your books and notebooks.
  • Do not disturb other students.
  • Keep the classroom clean.
  • Submit assignments on time.

Important Notice: Students must maintain the required attendance.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A class where the teacher explains a subject or topic.
Lab
A practical class where students perform experiments or programs.
Tutorial
A class for solving questions and understanding difficult topics.
Internal Assessment
Marks given during the semester through tests, assignments and activities.
Practical
A class where students apply their theoretical knowledge.
