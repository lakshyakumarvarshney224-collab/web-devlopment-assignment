Spandan - Annual College Fest 2026

Fest Introduction

Welcome to Spandan, the annual college fest of GLA University. Spandan is a celebration of talent, creativity, entertainment and teamwork. Students can participate in various competitions and exciting events.

Registration Deadline: 15 September 2026

Dear Students, participate actively in Spandan and show your talent, creativity and team spirit. We wish all participants the very best.

Fest Rules

  • Students must carry their college ID card.
  • Participants must report before the event starts.
  • Follow the instructions of event coordinators.
  • Maintain discipline at the venue.
  • Do not damage college property.
  • Participants must follow the event rules.

Important Fest Terms

Open Event
An event in which eligible students can participate.
Team Event
An event where students participate as a team.
Individual Event
An event where one student participates individually.
Registration
The process of signing up for a fest event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing an event.
Final Round
The last round of a competition where winners are selected.
