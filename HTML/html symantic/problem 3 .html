Types of Appointments

Consultation
A consultation is a meeting between a patient and healthcare professional.
Follow-up
A follow-up appointment is used to review previous treatment or progress.
OPD
OPD means Outpatient Department where patients can receive medical consultation.
Appointment
An appointment is a scheduled visit with a doctor or healthcare department.

Appointment Steps

  1. Choose a healthcare department.
  2. Select a doctor.
  3. Choose your preferred date and time.
  4. Enter your personal information.
  5. Enter the reason for your visit.
  6. Submit the appointment request.
