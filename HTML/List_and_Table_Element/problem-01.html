Admission Overview

GLA University Greater Noida provides various undergraduate and postgraduate programs for students.

Welcome to GLA University. Complete your admission application carefully and submit all required documents before the deadline.

Eligibility

Students must satisfy the eligibility requirements of the selected course.

Mandatory Requirement: Students must submit their required academic documents.

Application Deadline: 30 June 2026

Application Process

  1. Visit the official GLA University website.
  2. Register for admission.
  3. Fill the application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the application form.

Required Documents

  • 10th Marksheet
  • 12th Marksheet
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Identity Proof
  • Migration Certificate

Important Terms

Eligibility
The requirements that a student must meet for admission.
Merit List
A list of students selected according to their marks.
Counselling
The process of selecting a course or program.
Scholarship
Financial support given to eligible students.
Registration
The process of registering for admission.
