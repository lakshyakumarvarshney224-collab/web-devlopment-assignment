Spice Garden Restaurant

Welcome to Spice Garden Restaurant. We serve delicious Indian and international food prepared with fresh ingredients. Come and enjoy a wonderful dining experience with your family and friends.

Starters

  • Paneer Tikka - Grilled paneer with spices
  • Veg Spring Rolls - Crispy vegetable rolls
  • Chicken Tikka - Grilled chicken with Indian spices

Main Course

  • Butter Chicken - Chicken cooked in creamy tomato gravy
  • Paneer Butter Masala - Paneer in rich tomato gravy
  • Veg Biryani - Rice cooked with vegetables and spices
  • Chicken Biryani - Fragrant rice with chicken and spices

Desserts

  • Gulab Jamun - Sweet milk dumplings
  • Chocolate Brownie - Soft chocolate dessert
  • Ice Cream - Vanilla, Chocolate and Strawberry

Beverages

  • Mango Shake
  • Fresh Lime Soda
  • Masala Tea
  • Cold Coffee

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

  1. Start with Paneer Tikka or Chicken Tikka.
  2. Enjoy the Chef's Special Butter Chicken.
  3. Have Veg or Chicken Biryani with your main course.
  4. Finish your meal with Gulab Jamun.
  5. Enjoy a refreshing Mango Shake.
