One-Day Travel Itinerary

  1. Visit the Taj Mahal in the morning.
  2. Have lunch at a local restaurant.
  3. Visit Agra Fort in the afternoon.
  4. Explore local markets in the evening.
  5. Return to the hotel.

Travel Tips

  • Carry a valid identity document.
  • Keep enough drinking water with you.
  • Respect local culture and traditions.
  • Keep important documents safe.
  • Check the weather before travelling.
  • Keep emergency contact numbers available.
