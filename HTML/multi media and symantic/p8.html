Welcome to SBI Financial Education

Welcome to the State Bank of India banking and personal finance education portal.

Learn about bank accounts, debit cards, credit cards, loans, interest rates, EMI and responsible financial practices.

Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account helps customers safely keep money and earn interest on their savings.

Current Account

A current account is generally useful for businesses and customers who make frequent transactions.

Fixed Deposit

A fixed deposit allows customers to deposit money for a fixed period and earn interest.

Banking Cards

  • Debit Card
  • Credit Card
  • ATM Card
  • Contactless Card
  • International Card

Always protect your PIN, card details and banking passwords.

Loans

Home Loan

A home loan can help eligible customers purchase or construct a residential property.

Education Loan

An education loan can help eligible students meet expenses related to higher education.

Personal Loan

A personal loan may be used for eligible personal financial requirements.

Car Loan

A car loan can help eligible customers finance the purchase of a vehicle.

Important Financial Notice

Banking products, interest rates, charges and eligibility conditions may change.

Customers should check the official SBI information before making financial decisions.

Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Save money regularly.
  • Keep an emergency fund.
  • Pay loan EMIs on time.
  • Protect your banking information.
  • Avoid sharing your OTP and PIN.
  • Compare financial products before choosing them.
  • Plan your expenses carefully.

Account Opening Steps

  1. Select the type of account.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Complete the account opening form.
  4. Provide the required documents.
  5. Complete the verification process.
  6. Receive your account details.

Banking Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage used to calculate interest on a financial product.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid towards certain loans.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The period for which a loan or deposit is maintained.
